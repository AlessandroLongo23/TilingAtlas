\caption[The mismatch rule, worked on $3.4.4.6$]{Why the first of the four
local rules rejects what it rejects, worked on a $3.4.4.6$ vertex. (a) Each
half-edge sees one polygon on its left and one on its right, and that pair is
its \emph{profile}: reading clockwise from half-edge $0$, the four profiles are
hexagon/triangle, triangle/square, square/square and square/hexagon. (b)
Gluing two half-edges lays their polygons face to face, so the two profiles
must be opposites. The gluing $(0\;1)$ fails this: below the edge the two
triangles agree, but above it the hexagon flanking $0$ meets the square
flanking $1$, and no tiling contains that edge. (c) The only profile opposite
to hexagon/triangle is triangle/hexagon, which on this vertex is carried by
$*0$ alone, so $[0]$ is the single admissible gluing at half-edge $0$. The
rule is local, it is checked before any coordinate is computed, and
\cref{ch:engine-proof} must establish that it never rejects a partial assembly
that would have grown into a tiling.}
