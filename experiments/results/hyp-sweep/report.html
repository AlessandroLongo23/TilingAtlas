Hyperbolic tilings by uniformity, palette and valence

Every cell of the sweep enumerates the tilings of the hyperbolic plane built from regular {3…p}-gons with at most v edges at a vertex and at most k vertex orbits — identity decided by the minimal Delaney–Dress symbol of the quotient, so two entries differ iff the tilings genuinely differ. Boxes nest: a cell contains every smaller one, and counts are cumulative in k unless switched to exact below.

Reading the frontier

The censored band is where this enumeration currently ends, and it is a compute wall and not a mathematical one — every censored cell was still emitting raw blocks at the cap. Raising the cap moves the wall; nothing in the method changes. Regenerate this page after any new cell with node scripts/hyp-sweep-report.mjs.
